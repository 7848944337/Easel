About the Platform

Editorial independence, in service of the artist's hand.

We started Verdant & Vessel because the loudest voices in contemporary art are rarely the artists themselves. Our journal and awards exist to put craftsmanship, concept, and technique ahead of market hype — with a jury and editorial board answerable to no gallery, collector, or sponsor.

Every work we publish or award is made by a human hand. We verify process documentation for every submission, and we do not accept AI-generated imagery in any category.

Non-commercial by design

No entry-fee tiers that buy better odds, no pay-to-play sponsorships influencing the jury's decisions.

Human-made, verified

Authenticity declarations and process documentation are required for every painting and sculpture submitted.

Artist rights, protected

You retain full copyright always. We license only what's needed to publish and exhibit your work.

  • Image Specs
    Technical requirements for paintings

    300 DPI JPEGs minimum, accurate color representation, and even, glare-free lighting with no visible frame.

  • Sculpture Specs
    Requirements for 3D work

    Front, side, and back views plus one detail shot showing surface and texture, with a scale reference in at least one image.

  • Eligibility
    Originality & authenticity

    Work must be original, completed within the last three years, within published size limits, and accompanied by a signed non-AI declaration.

  • Copyright
    You keep 100% of your rights

    We take only a limited, non-exclusive license to publish and exhibit your entry — ownership never transfers to us.

Entries are reviewed anonymously in two stages: medium specialists score first, followed by a cross-panel review with the full jury before any award is finalized.
