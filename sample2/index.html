02 / About

Our position

Less noise.
More looking.

FORM / FIELD exists to make space for the inner journey, craftsmanship, concept and technique behind serious art.

Independent by design, we look beyond commercial market hype to document artists and practices with care. We champion human-made painting and physical sculpture, fair representation, artist rights and a healthier creative culture.
