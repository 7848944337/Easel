Copyright Notice

You keep your copyright

Artists retain full copyright over every work submitted to Plinth, whether to the magazine or the Plinth Prize. Submitting an entry does not transfer ownership, and we never resell, license to a third party, or claim any commercial right beyond what is described below.

By entering, you grant Plinth a limited, non-exclusive licence to reproduce your work for the purposes of award catalogues, the annual awards book, physical and digital exhibitions, and reasonable promotional use tied to that exhibition or publication. This licence ends when the associated catalogue or exhibition cycle concludes, unless separately renewed with your written consent.

If you believe your work has been used outside these terms, contact us immediately at the address on our Contact page and we will investigate and correct it.

Privacy Policy

How we handle your data

We collect only what's needed to process a submission, entry or inquiry: your name, contact details, and the materials you send us. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with third parties except where required to run the award (for example, sending shortlisted entries to jurors) or where legally required.

During blind jury evaluation, entries are stripped of identifying information before they reach first-round jurors. Names are reattached only after scoring is complete, for the cross-panel and final stages.

Plinth processes personal data in line with applicable data protection law, including GDPR for entrants based in the European Economic Area. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by writing to us.

Terms of Participation

Rules for entrants and contributors

All work submitted to the magazine or the Plinth Prize must be original and made by the entrant. Work that is found to misrepresent its authorship, medium, or creation date — including undeclared AI-generated content — will be disqualified, and repeated violations may result in a ban from future cycles.

Entrants must be the sole rights-holder of any submitted work, or have documented permission from all rights-holders. Plinth is not responsible for disputes between co-authors or collaborators that arise after publication or exhibition.

Disputes relating to jury decisions, eligibility, or these terms are handled first through direct correspondence with our editorial team; unresolved disputes are subject to the laws of Portugal, where Plinth Publications is registered.
