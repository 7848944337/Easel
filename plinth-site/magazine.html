Volume 14 — Interior Weather

What changes when nobody else is watching the work happen

Eleven painters and six sculptors on the domestic and the unfinished — kitchen tables mid-sitting, studios at closing time, sculpture still warm from the cast.

Cover artist Mara Voss talks through eight years of returning to the same window light, and critic Adaeze Okonkwo asks whether "finished" is a useful word for a painting at all.
