Volume 14 — On Newsstands Now

Interior Weather

This issue sits inside the domestic and the unfinished — kitchen tables mid-sitting, studios at closing time, sculpture still warm from the cast. Eleven painters and six sculptors on what changes when nobody else is watching the work happen.

Cover artist Mara Voss talks through eight years of returning to the same window light, and critic Adaeze Okonkwo asks whether "finished" is a useful word for a painting at all.
