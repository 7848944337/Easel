What We're Looking For

Contribution types

Choose the format that fits what you want to say.

Visual Portfolios / Series

5–10 related works, accompanied by a short concept statement.

  • 200–300 word statement
  • High-resolution images, medium & year noted
  • A brief note on the connecting thread

Single Standout Artworks

One work of exceptional depth, for editorial illustration.

  • One high-resolution image
  • Title, medium, dimensions, year
  • A sentence or two of context

Essays & Critical Writing

First-person essays, technical articles, and art-historical reflections, 800–1,500 words.

  • Studio practice essays
  • Technique & materials writing
  • Criticism and art history
  • Legal or professional-practice topics
