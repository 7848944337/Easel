About Plinth

An independent home for painters and sculptors

Founded in 2016 by a working painter and a foundry-trained sculptor, Plinth exists to give both disciplines equal, unhurried attention — in print and in a prize that answers to a jury, not a market.

Mission & Philosophy

What we're actually trying to do

Four commitments that shape every editorial and jury decision we make.

01

Craft over hype

We ask about pigment, armature, glaze and grain before we ask about market comparables. Concept and technique carry an entry, not a collector's name.

02

Editorial independence

Plinth takes no gallery advertising in exchange for editorial coverage, and no juror scores work from an artist they represent commercially.

03

Two disciplines, one table

Most prizes treat sculpture as painting's smaller sibling. We run separate specialist juries for each, with equal prize value and equal page count.

04

Artists keep everything

Entrants retain full copyright at every stage. We license only what's needed to print, exhibit and promote — nothing more, nothing permanent.

What Sets Us Apart

Made by hand, judged by hand

Every entry to Plinth is a physical, human-made work — traditional or contemporary in method, but never AI-generated. Entrants sign a declaration to that effect, and our jury is trained to flag work that doesn't hold up under close, in-person inspection.

We also take artist wellbeing seriously as an editorial subject, not just a value statement: every issue carries at least one piece on the practical realities of a working studio practice, burnout included.

Submission Guidelines

What we need from you

Please read closely before you upload — incomplete entries are not sent to jury.

01

Image specifications

300 DPI JPEGs, colour-accurate and evenly lit. No visible frame or wall unless the frame is part of the work itself.

02

Sculpture documentation

Four views minimum — front, side, back — plus one surface-detail shot with a scale reference in frame.

03

Eligibility

Work must be original, completed within the last three years, and fall within the category's stated size limits. A signed non-AI declaration is required for every entry.

04

Copyright terms

Entrants retain 100% of the intellectual property in their work. Plinth is granted a limited, non-exclusive licence to reproduce entries for catalogue, exhibition and promotional use only.
